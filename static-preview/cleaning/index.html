A new client from a referral and a new client from a pay-per-lead marketplace are the same work. What changes is how many leads you paid for to win one.

At two new clients a month, winning them from referrals and your own reviews instead of paid leads is about $3,400 a year kept (24 clients × $140).

Step 5 · Being seen

Nobody hires the cleaning company with four reviews and a dead feed

People pick a cleaner from Google in about a minute: the rating, the review count, and whether the photos look like real homes. That is the whole audition.

We ask every client how it went

Most cleaning companies have a handful of reviews because asking feels awkward and the moment passes. Fynz asks for you, once, the evening of the clean.

One request, the evening of the clean

Sent once, in your company's name, with the Google link. A client walking into a clean house is the most willing reviewer you will ever have.

No gating, ever

Every client is asked, not only the happy ones. That keeps you inside Google's rules against review gating, and your reviews read as real because they are.
